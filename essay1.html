What happens to the questions nobody lets you ask ?

They don't disappear. They just go quiet

In an age where every answer is a search away, what students are slowly losing is not knowledge but the courage to feel and question.

To sit with something unresolved, to ask a question which probably has no clear answers waiting on the other side, Every student carries these questions about who they are, what they feel, what they notice about the world that no else seems to see. But unfortunately schools don't usually have space for these questions. Ironically neither does social media with all its noise and its endless void. so students learn to swallow them and something essential dies in that silence.

Vishvakalpa exists because we believe that silence is not inevitable. Cave sessions are our answer, inspired by the student led sacred gatherings in the movie Dead Poets Society but built for every student not just poets, A place for students from any background speaking any language, working in any domain can bring that swallowed question into the Open and find that the doors don't get shut. A poem in their mother tongue or maybe in a foreign language they are not probably friendlier with, be it complete or incomplete, A photograph with a story, a Question from a textbook which was never asked, all are equally welcome.

No grades, No judgement, No right or wrong answers. For students, the impact is quiet but profound. They discover that their language, their experience, their inner world have value and slowly they begin to ask better questions. Which is the only education that truly lasts. For everyone, Cave sessions are proof that revolutions doesn't always look like war and candle march, sometimes it looks like few students sitting together be it online or offline. finally being heard perhaps for the very first time.

For every out-of-the-box question you have, for every piece of knowledge that astounds you, for each verse of the writings you hide in your diary, we are here for you.
Vishvakalpa provides you with the words you need to frame your opinion.
